# The Most Boring Object, Reviewed in One Line

A one-line review of the doorstop — the most overlooked object in any room. It spends its whole life being kicked, stepped on and forgotten, yet still holds the door open for people who never once said thank you. The joke is the object's own dullness: a doorstop's entire existence is being walked over in service of a door nobody thanks it for.
